# Calendar template

Show the calendar as one table with one row per slot, in date order. Use the dates of the coming week.

| Day | Pillar | Format | Signal | Angle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon 12 Oct | [pillar title] | linkedin | [signal title](articleUrl), id `[signal id]` | [one sentence that takes a position] | [why this signal, this pillar and this day] |
| Tue 13 Oct | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

After the table, add these two short sections.

## Pillar coverage

List each active pillar with its number of slots, for example:

- [pillar title]: 2 slots
- [pillar title]: 1 slot
- [pillar title]: 0 slots. No signal this week touched it.

## Not used

List up to five strong signals that the plan left out, each with one line on why. For example: "Covers the same event as Monday's slot" or "Fits no active pillar".

## Column rules

- **Day**: the weekday and the date. Put the freshest news first.
- **Pillar**: the pillar title exactly as the profile spells it.
- **Format**: one of `linkedin`, `x`, `long_form`, `email_prospect` or `email_journalist`.
- **Signal**: the signal title linked to its article, and the signal id. The draft-post skill needs the id.
- **Angle**: one sentence in the company's voice that states the position the post takes.
- **Why**: one line. Name the urgency, the publish date, or the tracked company that earns the slot.
